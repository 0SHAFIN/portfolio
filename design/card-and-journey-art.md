# Distinct card artwork and the scroll journey

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Every work card and every career card has its own original illustration; none uses a panel from the old shared triptych. The journey horizon is a seventh, separate scene retained as a design source. The live journey uses the requested original pink background and cream postcard theme. Original PNGs are retained in `design/source-art`; optimized WebP assets are copied into `public/art`.

Scroll progress moves Byte from checkpoint 1 to 3, fills the trail, and adds a small parallax shift to the postcard illustrations. All three cream cards stay visible in a desktop row on the original rose background. On smaller screens they stack vertically while the progress trail stays at the top as the cards scroll past. Byte uses the isolated, padded running atlas and faces the scroll direction; the pose animation runs only during scrolling. Checkpoint buttons provide direct navigation. Global pause freezes the journey, and reduced motion leaves an ordinary readable section. Every card's links stay available to keyboard users. Scroll listeners, pending frames, and idle timers are cleaned up.
